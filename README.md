# Metallurgical Laboratory Internship — ITRAC

## 1. Overview

This repository presents my summer internship experience at the Automotive Parts and Assemblies Testing and Research Company (ITRAC), with a focus on metallurgical laboratory practices, materials characterization, and quality assessment of automotive components.

The internship provided an opportunity to explore laboratory testing procedures, metallographic examination, and the role of materials engineering in evaluating industrial components.

## 2. Internship Activities and Technical Topics

### Metallography and Microstructural Examination
- Metallographic sample preparation
- Sectioning, mounting, wet grinding, and polishing
- Microstructural examination of metallic materials
- Metallography of cast irons and other engineering materials
- Understanding the importance of sample preparation in reliable microstructural analysis

### Materials Testing and Characterization
- Overview of mechanical and chemical testing methods
- Evaluation of metallic materials and automotive components
- Understanding laboratory testing procedures and relevant standards
- Interpretation of metallurgical test results

### Industrial Quality Assessment
- The role of laboratory testing in automotive quality control
- Assessment of material properties and microstructural characteristics
- The importance of reliable test procedures in evaluating component quality
- Introduction to metallurgical investigation and failure analysis

## 3. Key Learning Outcomes

- Understanding practical metallurgical laboratory workflows
- Recognizing the relationship between sample preparation and analysis quality
- Developing familiarity with materials characterization techniques
- Understanding the role of standardized testing in industrial quality assurance
- Connecting theoretical materials science knowledge with industrial applications

## 4. Technical Skills

- Metallographic sample preparation
- Microstructure examination
- Materials characterization
- Metallurgical testing procedures
- Technical data interpretation
- Laboratory documentation and scientific reporting
